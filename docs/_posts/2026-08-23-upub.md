title: uPub — write books on your phone
---

[uPub](/upub/) is a new unifile type: a distraction-free Markdown writing app, built for phones first, that exports real e-books. (It launched under the working name "Writer".)

It is deliberately not the Markdown editor we already ship. That one is a *document tool* — panes, live preview, code-editor guts. uPub is a *writing tool*: one column of text, dimmed syntax markers, a keyboard toolbar, and nothing else on screen.

## A different editor

uPub doesn't use CodeMirror. Its editor renders each source line as its own block, which buys the one typographic feature writing apps live or die by: **hanging indent**. When a list item wraps, the wrapped lines align under the item's text — not under the bullet. The editor font is monospaced, so the indent is character-exact, like a well-set outline.

Everything else stays plain text. What you see *is* the file; markers are just quieter than words.

## EPUB out

Export produces a standard **EPUB 3** (with an EPUB 2 fallback TOC), assembled entirely on your device: every `#` heading becomes a chapter, `##` headings land in the reader's table of contents, front matter (`title:`, `author:`, `language:` …) fills the book's metadata, and a title page is generated. On iOS the share sheet hands it straight to Apple Books or Files.

## Still a unifile

The rest is the unifile you know: git-style commits and restore inside the document, a `.unifile.json` that round-trips text plus full history, an installable offline PWA and a single-file `.html` shape, and a strict no-network, no-telemetry posture.
